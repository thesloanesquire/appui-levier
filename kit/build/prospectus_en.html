Where your weeks go

“We don't have time to apply.”

A rejected application cost 32 hours on average, an accepted one 63 (2019 data). That time is then missing from the project.

“We don't know which foundations to write to.”

Switzerland has 13,782 public-benefit foundations (end of 2025). Only some of them give grants, and some accept no unsolicited applications.

“Rejections come with no explanation.”

A late or incomplete file is set aside without review, and one outside the purpose often is too. The rejection gives no reason, and you start again without knowing what to change.

These rejections have causes that can be fixed: a purpose that does not cover the project, a template letter, a submission after the deadline.

A missed meeting delays the project by 3 to 6 months.

Several large foundations decide two to four times a year and want the file about four months before the project starts. A project that has already begun is generally no longer funded.

Our guarantee

If we do not find at least 15 foundations whose purpose matches your project, the Diagnostic is free. The decision rests with each foundation's board.

No commission. The application stays yours.

Swissfundraising rules out commissions on funds obtained (ethical principles, ch. 4), and the Ernst Göhner Foundation refuses applications tied to a commission. Levier charges fixed fees; your organisation signs and sends.
