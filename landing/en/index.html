Does this sound familiar?

“We don't have time for applications.”

Each application takes dozens of hours, often in the evening on volunteer time. Meanwhile, the project waits.

“We don't know which foundations to write to.”

Switzerland has over 13,000 charitable foundations. Most will never fund your project, and that is written nowhere.

“Rejections come with no explanation.”

Off-purpose, late, too generic: foundations set these aside. You start again without knowing why.

The problem is not your project. It is applications sent to the wrong foundations, with generic letters, too late.

One missed board meeting means six months of delay.

Most foundations decide two to four times a year and want your file about four months before the project starts. A project already under way is usually no longer funded.

Enter a date: we work out when to apply.

Rule of thumb from rules published by several foundations (about 4 months before the start, 5 for some large foundations). Each foundation has its own dates: we check them one by one.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you guarantee funding?

    No one can: the decision belongs to each foundation's board. We guarantee the work: foundations whose purpose fits, a letter written for each, deadlines kept.

    Why not a percentage of funds raised?

    Because foundations refuse to fund commissions and Swissfundraising's ethical principles rule them out. With fixed fees, you know what you pay.

    Don't foundations dislike agencies?

    They dislike commissions and form letters. Here: no commission, one letter per foundation, signed and sent by your organisation.

    Do you use artificial intelligence?

    To search and check: reading the purposes of thousands of foundations, spotting deadlines. Every letter is reviewed and adjusted by a person, then approved by you.

    What do you need from us?

    The project budget, your statutes and latest accounts, a short presentation of your organisation. We tell you from the start which documents each foundation requires.

    How long until a reply?

    Usually three to six months, depending on board meetings. Hence starting at least six months before the project begins.
